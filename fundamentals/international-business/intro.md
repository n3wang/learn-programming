# International Business

Trade across borders is a **systems** problem: contracts, documents, money, transport, and law have to line up, or the physical goods never “count” as a completed deal. This section teaches export–import practice the same way we teach engineering fundamentals — clear definitions, risk models, and interactive drills — in **original** lesson prose (not a reprint of any commercial handbook).

### How we build lessons here
1. **Outline** topics from reputable public sources (CISG text, ICC Incoterms® summaries you paraphrase, UCP concepts, your own notes).
2. **Rewrite** in teaching voice — never paste handbook chapters or long case excerpts.
3. **Interact** — guided choices, checklists, and (later) document-matching simulators.
4. **Wiki** — catalog reusable procedures (e.g. “assemble a documentary pack”) when a pattern stabilizes.
