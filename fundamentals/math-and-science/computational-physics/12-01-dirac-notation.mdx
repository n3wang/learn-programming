# 12.1 Dirac notation in quantum mechanics

> **Source:** Ideas from *Computational Physics* (Landau, Páez, Bordeianu), Ch. 12 opener + §12.1 (G. He, coauthor). Rewritten in original wording. Continues [11.11b](./11-11b-nn-code-labs). Next: [12.1b](./12-01b-dirac-notation-labs).

Quantum computing (QC) speaks Dirac. This chapter builds qubits, entanglement, and gates, then (later) Cirq / IBM hardware. Programs stay low-level — gates on bits — much like early classical machines. Sources for going deeper: Hidary; Stolze & Suter; Nielsen & Chuang; IBM Quantum; Cirq.

**Problem (chapter arc):** write programs that store and process information in quantum states.

## Kets, bras, brackets

A state is a **ket** $|\psi\rangle$ — a ray in a complex Hilbert space (dimension $2^n$ for $n$ qubits). The dual **bra** is the adjoint $\langle\psi|=|\psi\rangle^{\dagger}$. Pick the inner-product identity and the projector $|0\rangle\langle 0|$ among lookalikes.

<GuidedChoiceExplanation preset="qc-dirac-ket" />

The wave function is the coordinate projection $\psi(x)=\langle x\mid\psi\rangle$. Outer product $|\phi\rangle\langle\psi|$ is an **operator** (matrix), not a scalar: it maps states to states.

## Spin-½ ↔ computational basis

QC renames spin-up / spin-down:

$$
|0\rangle\equiv\bigl|+\tfrac12\bigr\rangle=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
|1\rangle\equiv\bigl|-\tfrac12\bigr\rangle=\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

(Hyperfine / spin matrices appeared in §7.6.)

## Takeaway

| Object | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| $|\psi\rangle$ | State vector (ket) |
| $\langle\psi|$ | Dual (bra) |
| $\langle\phi\mid\psi\rangle$ | Inner product |
| $|\phi\rangle\langle\psi|$ | Operator |
| $|0\rangle,|1\rangle$ | Qubit computational basis |
