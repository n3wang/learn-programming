## Write-ups

<ProblemSet id="cp-86b-assess">
  <Problem
    type="concept"
    title="8.6b.W1"
    minWords={3}
    keywords={['under', 'critical', 'over']}
    prompt={`Classify free motion for b compared with 2mω₀ (three regimes).`}
    answer={`Underdamped if b<2mω₀ (rings), critical if equal (fastest non-oscillatory return), overdamped if b>2mω₀ (creep).`}
  />
  <Problem
    type="concept"
    title="8.6b.W2"
    minWords={3}
    keywords={['resonance', 'damping', 'peak']}
    prompt={`What happens to the driven amplitude peak as viscous damping increases?`}
    answer={`The resonance peak lowers and broadens (and shifts slightly); light damping gives a sharp peak near ω₀.`}
  />
  <Problem
    type="concept"
    title="8.6b.W3"
    minWords={3}
    keywords={['beat', 'transient', 'drive']}
    prompt={`When do beats appear in a lightly damped driven oscillator?`}
    answer={`When a transient near ω₀ coexists with a steady drive at a nearby ω, their interference produces a slow envelope at |ω−ω₀|/(2π).`}
  />
  <Problem
    type="concept"
    title="8.6b.W4"
    minWords={3}
    keywords={['nonlinear', 'amplitude', 'resonance']}
    prompt={`How can a nonlinear restoring force change a resonance curve versus the linear case?`}
    answer={`Amplitude-dependent period bends the peak (and can cause jumps); the linear Lorentzian shape no longer applies.`}
  />
</ProblemSet>
