<Exercise title="9.1b.3 Partial sum at T/4" anchor="exercise-cp-91-partial">

:::tip Activity: Truncated sawtooth
With $\omega t=\pi/2$ (i.e. $t=T/4$), sum
$y_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\dfrac{2}{n\pi}(-1)^{n+1}\sin(n\omega t)$
for $N=4$. Print with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Sawtooth partial N=4 at T/4"
  heading="exercise-cp-91-partial"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="Sawtooth partial sum N=4 at ωt=π/2; 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`0.4244131816`}
  starter={`import math

N = 4
wt = math.pi / 2.0
y = 0.0
# TODO: accumulate terms n=1..N
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{y:.10f}")
`}
  hint="True ramp value is 0.5; N=4 is still a coarse Fourier fit."
  solution={`import math

N = 4
wt = math.pi / 2.0
y = 0.0
for n in range(1, N + 1):
    bn = (2.0 / (n * math.pi)) * ((-1) ** (n + 1))
    y += bn * math.sin(n * wt)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'loops harmonics', pattern: 'for\\s+', must: true, hint: 'sum n=1..N'},
    {name: 'uses sin', pattern: 'sin\\s*\\(', must: true, hint: 'bn*sin(n*wt)'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'partial N4', equals: '0.4244131816'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>
