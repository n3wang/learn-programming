No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Daughter s=1/2"
  heading="exercise-cp-104-daughter"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="s=0.5, τ=0, t=π/32; print ψ; 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`1.3872134450`}
  starter={`import math

s, tau = 0.5, 0.0

  wrapSuffix={`

  hint="u=π/16; factor √2 multiplies Ψ(u)."
  solution={`import math

t = math.pi / 32.0

psi = Psi / math.sqrt(s)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'scales arg', pattern: '/\\s*s|s\\s*\\)', must: true, hint: '(t−τ)/s'},

## Sketch (structure only)

```python
# cwt_sketch.py — original structure (not a textbook dump)
# Y[i_s, i_tau] ≈ sum_k psi_s_tau(t_k) * y[k] * dt
for s in scales:
    for tau in taus:
        acc = 0.0
        for t, yt in zip(ts, y):
            acc += daughter(t, s, tau) * yt * dt
        Y[(s, tau)] = acc
```
