# 11.3 Training and backpropagation

> **Source:** Ideas from *Computational Physics* (Landau, Páez, Bordeianu), §11.2.3–11.2.5. Rewritten in original wording — **no** textbook `SimpleNet.py` dump. Continues [11.2b](./11-02b-simple-nn-labs). Next: [11.3b](./11-03b-training-labs).

## Teach with known answers

![Figure 11.4 — forward → Loss → ∂ℒ → update.](/img/computational-physics/fig-11-4-train-flow.svg)

Feed training pairs with known correct outputs $y^{(c)}$. Compare to predictions $y^{(p)}$. Define **Loss**, nudge weights with derivatives (**backpropagation**), then repeat. Pick MSE, the SGD step, and the logistic $f'=f(1-f)$ identity among lookalikes.

Finally evaluate on **unseen** data. Trained nets are efficient pattern engines but poor explainers: the parameter soup rarely narrates *why* a decision was made (hence the Blueprint quote in §11.1).

## Meson-track toy

Classify $\pi$ ($1$) vs $\mu$ ($0$) from track length/width. Mean-center features (length mean $14$, width mean $8$):

| ID | Length$-14$ | Width$-8$ | Label |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | $-1$ | $-2$ | $1$ ($\pi$) |
| B | $2$ | $2$ | $0$ ($\mu$) |
| C | $1$ | $1$ | $0$ |
| D | $-2$ | $-1$ | $1$ |

If the net always predicts $0$, then $\mathcal{L}=0.5$ on these four labels — right half the time by chance.

## Gradients via the chain rule

Extremize $\mathcal{L}$ in the weights/biases (six $w$'s and three $b$'s even in the tiny 2–2–1 net): seek $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial w_i=0$ and $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial b_j=0$. In practice, compute gradients and take the SGD steps you picked above.

Example (book walk-through style): one sample with $x_1=-2$, $x_2=-1$, $y^{(c)}=1$, all weights $1$, biases $0$:

$$
h_1=h_2=f(-3)\approx 0.0474,\qquad
y^{(p)}=f(2h_1)\approx 0.524.
$$

Then

$$
\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial w_1}
=\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial y^{(p)}}
\frac{\partial y^{(p)}}{\partial h_1}
\frac{\partial h_1}{\partial w_1}
\approx 0.0215
$$

(positive ⇒ decrease $w_1$ to lower $\mathcal{L}$). Sweep parameters one-at-a-time or in batches until $\mathcal{L}$ stalls.

## Practice checklist

1. Implement MSE Loss; check $\mathcal{L}=0.5$ for all-zero predictions on the four labels.
2. Forward the all-ones-weight net on $(x_1,x_2)=(-2,-1)$; lock $y^{(p)}$.
3. Compute $f'(-3)$ and $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial w_1$; apply one SGD step and re-forward.
4. (Open) Train a 2–2–1 net on all four tracks; try 3 hidden nodes or two hidden layers and compare final $\mathcal{L}$.

## Takeaway

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Forward | Predict $y^{(p)}$ |
| Loss | MSE vs $y^{(c)}$ |
| Backward | Chain-rule $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial w$ |
| Update | $w\leftarrow w-\eta\,\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial w$ |
| Test | Unseen tracks / holdout |
