<Exercise title="8.5b.1 RK2 one step on F=−x³" anchor="exercise-cp-85-rk2-x3">

:::tip Activity: Anharmonic micro-step
From $(x,v)=(0.5,0)$, take one RK2 step with $h=0.01$ and $F=-x^{3}$ ($m=1$). Print `x v` with 10 decimals.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="RK2 one step on F=-x³"
  heading="exercise-cp-85-rk2-x3"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="One RK2 step h=0.01 from (0.5,0) with F=-x³; print x v 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`0.4999937500 -0.0012500000`}
  starter={`h = 0.01
x, v = 0.5, 0.0

def f(x, v):
    return v, -(x ** 3)

# TODO: one midpoint RK2 step
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{x:.10f} {v:.10f}")
`}
  hint="Same RK2 as §8.4b; only f₁ = −x³ changes."
  solution={`h = 0.01
x, v = 0.5, 0.0

def f(x, v):
    return v, -(x ** 3)

f0, f1 = f(x, v)
k1x, k1v = h * f0, h * f1
f0, f1 = f(x + 0.5 * k1x, v + 0.5 * k1v)
k2x, k2v = h * f0, h * f1
x, v = x + k2x, v + k2v
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'uses x**3 force', pattern: '\\*\\*\\s*3|x\\s*\\*\\s*x\\s*\\*\\s*x', must: true, hint: 'force −x³'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'nl rk2 step', equals: '0.4999937500 -0.0012500000'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>

<Exercise title="8.5b.2 Harmonic energy place" anchor="exercise-cp-85-E0">

:::tip Activity: Harmonic E₀
For $m=1$, $k=(2\pi)^{2}$, $(x,v)=(0,1)$, print initial energy $E_0=\tfrac12 v^{2}+\tfrac12 k x^{2}$ with 6 decimals.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Harmonic E0"
  heading="exercise-cp-85-E0"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="Print E0=½v²+½kx² for (0,1), k=(2π)²; 6 decimals."
  sampleLog={`0.500000`}
  starter={`import math

k = (2.0 * math.pi) ** 2
x, v = 0.0, 1.0
E0 = 0.0  # TODO
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{E0:.6f}")
`}
  hint="At x=0, E=½v²=0.5."
  solution={`import math

k = (2.0 * math.pi) ** 2
x, v = 0.0, 1.0
E0 = 0.5 * v * v + 0.5 * k * x * x
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'uses energy pieces', pattern: '0\\.5|1\\s*/\\s*2', must: true, hint: 'form ½mv²+½kx²'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'E0', equals: '0.500000'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>

  <Problem

    prompt={`Why is monitoring E still useful for F=−x³ even though the motion is anharmonic?`}
    answer={`A time-independent potential still conserves mechanical energy; drift in E diagnoses integrator error, not anharmonicity.`}
  />
  <Problem
    type="concept"
    title="8.5b.W3"

    keywords={['p', 'harmonic', 'isochronous']}
    prompt={`For V∝|x|^p, when is the period independent of amplitude?`}
    answer={`Only for p=2 (the harmonic well); other even p give amplitude-dependent periods.`}
  />
  <Problem

    prompt={`What goes wrong if a soft-α trajectory climbs past x=1/α?`}
