# 12.8 Quantum Fourier transform

> **Source:** Ideas from *Computational Physics* (Landau, Páez, Bordeianu), §12.8. Original wording — **no** textbook Qiskit listing dumps. Continues [12.7b](./12-07b-qiskit-labs). Labs: [12.8b](./12-08b-qft-labs).

Chapter 9’s DFT maps a length-$N$ signal $y_k$ to coefficients $Y_n$ with powers of $Z_N=e^{-2\pi i/N}$. The **quantum Fourier transform** does the same job on an $N=2^n$-dimensional Hilbert space. Pick the QFT definition and the $\mathrm{QFT}_4$ matrix among lookalikes.

<GuidedChoiceExplanation preset="qc-qft" />

(Qiskit / CS indexing from $0$; no extra $\sqrt{2\pi}$ factors.) Basis labels: $|0\rangle=|00\rangle$, $|1\rangle=|01\rangle$, $|2\rangle=|10\rangle$, $|3\rangle=|11\rangle$.

## Gate factorization (2 qubits)

With Hadamard $H$ and phase $P(\theta)=\mathrm{diag}(1,e^{i\theta})$, and $Z\equiv Z_4=-i$,

$$
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{QFT}_4|00\rangle&=H|0\rangle\otimes H|0\rangle,\\
\mathrm{QFT}_4|01\rangle&=H|1\rangle\otimes P(\pi/2)H|0\rangle,\\
\mathrm{QFT}_4|10\rangle&=H|0\rangle\otimes H|1\rangle,\\
\mathrm{QFT}_4|11\rangle&=H|1\rangle\otimes P(\pi/2)H|1\rangle.
\end{align*}
$$

Compactly (then **SWAP** the two output lines to match textbook wire order):

$$
\mathrm{QFT}_4|q_1 q_0\rangle
\;\widehat=\;
H|q_0\rangle\otimes P^{q_0}(\pi/2)\,H|q_1\rangle
\quad\text{(+ SWAP)}.
$$

## Sampling exercise (idea)

Take four samples of $y(t)=3\cos(\omega t)+2\cos(3\omega t)$, apply $\mathrm{QFT}_4$, read frequency bins, and check that the inverse (or direct sum of components) rebuilds $y$. Labs lock $Z_4=-i$ and the $(0,0)$ entry $1/2$.

## $n$-qubit QFT

With $n$ qubits, $N=2^n$ components live in an $N$-D Hilbert space (tensor-product basis). The circuit generalizes: layered Hadamards and controlled phase rotations $R_k=P(2\pi/2^k)$, then bit-reversal SWAPs — still $O(n^2)$ gates vs classical $O(N\log N)$ on explicitly stored vectors.

## Takeaway

| Classical DFT | QFT |
| --- | --- |
| $Z_N^{nk}$ on arrays | same phases on amplitudes |
| $O(N\log N)$ FFT | $O(n^2)$ gates, $N=2^n$ |
| QFT₄ | $H$, controlled-$P(\pi/2)$, SWAP |
