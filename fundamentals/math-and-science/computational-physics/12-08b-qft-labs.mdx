<Exercise title="12.8b.1 Z₄ = −i" anchor="exercise-cp-128-z">

:::tip Activity: Imaginary part
$Z_4=e^{-i\pi/2}=-i$. Print real and imag parts space-separated with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Z4"
  heading="exercise-cp-128-z"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="0 -1"

`}

`}
  hint="Should be 0 − 1j."
  solution={`import cmath
import math

z = cmath.exp(-1j * math.pi / 2)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'exp', pattern: 'exp', must: true, hint: 'complex exp'},

    {name: 'z', equals: '0.0000000000 -1.0000000000'},

/>
