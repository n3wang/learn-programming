<CodeExercise

    0.0, 0.511, 105.65, 105.658, 134.98, 139.57, 139.57,

z = 0.0  # TODO (0.511 - mu) / s
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{z:.10f}")
`}
  hint="Center then divide by sample std."
  solution={`import math

masses = [
    0.0, 0.511, 105.65, 105.658, 134.98, 139.57, 139.57,
