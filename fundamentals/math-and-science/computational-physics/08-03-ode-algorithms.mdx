# 8.4 ODE algorithms

> **Source:** Ideas from *Computational Physics* (Landau, Páez, Bordeianu), §8.4. Original wording — **not** a paste of `rk4.py` / `rk45.py` / `ABM.py`. Continues [8.2–8.3](./08-02-ode-form-and-review). Labs: [8.4b ODE algorithm labs](./08-03b-ode-algorithms-labs).

## Fixed steps

We march with a uniform step $h$ (Fig. 8.5). Adaptive and multistep methods appear only as structure sketches in [8.7](./08-06-ode-sketches).

![Figure 8.5 — uniform time steps.](/img/computational-physics/fig-8-5-uniform-steps.svg)

## Euler and midpoint RK2

Euler follows the left-endpoint tangent; midpoint RK2 samples a half-step slope. Walk both updates among lookalike formulas (RK4 stages are separate below).

## Classic RK4 (structure)

Four slopes with weights $1/6$, $1/3$, $1/3$, $1/6$. Pick the stage definitions and the weighted update among lookalikes.

<GuidedChoiceExplanation preset="rk4-weights" />

Labs implement RK2 carefully and may use this RK4 structure; do **not** paste VPython textbook listings.

## What we leave for sketches

- **RK45 (adaptive):** two embedded formulas estimate the local error and adjust $h$.
- **Adams–Bashforth–Moulton (ABM):** multistep predictor–corrector using a history of $\mathbf f$ values.

Both are outlined in [8.7](./08-06-ode-sketches) without dumping publisher code.
