<Exercise title="9.4c.1 Lowpass |H|" anchor="exercise-cp-944-lp">

:::tip Activity: RC lowpass gain
For $H=1/(1+i\omega\tau)$ at $\omega\tau=1$, print $|H|=1/\sqrt{1+(\omega\tau)^2}$ with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Lowpass |H|"
  heading="exercise-cp-944-lp"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="ωτ=1; print |H_lp|=1/sqrt(1+(ωτ)²) with 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`0.7071067812`}
  starter={`import math

wt = 1.0
mag = 0.0  # TODO
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{mag:.10f}")
`}
  hint="1/√2 at ωτ=1."
  solution={`import math

wt = 1.0
mag = 1.0 / math.sqrt(1.0 + wt * wt)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'uses sqrt', pattern: 'sqrt', must: true, hint: 'magnitude formula'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'lp mag', equals: '0.7071067812'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>

<Exercise title="9.4c.3 Two-tap smoother" anchor="exercise-cp-944-taps">

<CodeExercise
  title="Two-tap filter sample"
  heading="exercise-cp-944-taps"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="c=[0.5,0.5], f=[1,2,3]; print g1=c0*f1+c1*f0; 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`1.5000000000`}

</Exercise>
