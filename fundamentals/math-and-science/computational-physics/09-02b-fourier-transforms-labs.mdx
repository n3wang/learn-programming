<Exercise title="9.2b.1 Normalization factor" anchor="exercise-cp-92-norm">

:::tip Activity: $1/\sqrt{2\pi}$
Print $1/\sqrt{2\pi}$ with **10 decimals** (physics FT convention).

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="FT normalization"
  heading="exercise-cp-92-norm"

`}
  wrapSuffix={`

`}
  hint="math.sqrt(2*math.pi) in the denominator."

`}

/>

<Exercise title="9.2b.2 Power from complex Y" anchor="exercise-cp-92-power">

:::tip Activity: $|Y|^2$
Given $Y=3-4i$, print the power $|Y|^2=\mathrm{Re}^2+\mathrm{Im}^2$ with **2 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Complex power"
  heading="exercise-cp-92-power"

  filename="main.py"

  wrapSuffix={`

`}

`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'squares', pattern: '\\*', must: true, hint: 're*re + im*im'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'power', equals: '25.00'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>
