<Exercise title="9.4b.1 Discrete A(0)" anchor="exercise-cp-94-a0">

:::tip Activity: Autocorr at lag 0
For $N=16$ samples $s_k=1/(1-0.9\sin(2\pi k/N))$, the discrete autocorrelation at lag $0$ is $A_0=\sum_k s_k^2$. Print $A_0$ with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Autocorr lag 0"
  heading="exercise-cp-94-a0"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="N=16, s=1/(1-0.9 sin(2πk/N)); print sum(s**2) with 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`194.9424983618`}
  starter={`import math

N = 16
A0 = 0.0
# TODO: sum s_k**2
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{A0:.10f}")
`}
  hint="Loop k=0..15; s=1/(1-0.9*sin(...))."
  solution={`import math

N = 16
A0 = 0.0
for k in range(N):
    s = 1.0 / (1.0 - 0.9 * math.sin(2.0 * math.pi * k / N))
    A0 += s * s
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'loops', pattern: 'for\\s+', must: true, hint: 'sum over k'},
    {name: 'has 0.9', pattern: '0\\.9', must: true, hint: 'use the 0.9 factor'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'A0', equals: '194.9424983618'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>

<Exercise title="9.4b.3 Noise model sample" anchor="exercise-cp-94-noise">

:::tip Activity: One noisy sample
With $s=2$, $\alpha=0.5$, $r=0.25$, form $y=s+\alpha(2r-1)$. Print $y$ with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="One noisy sample"
  heading="exercise-cp-94-noise"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="y=s+α(2r−1) with s=2, α=0.5, r=0.25; 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`1.7500000000`}
  starter={`s = 2.0
alpha = 0.5
r = 0.25
y = 0.0  # TODO
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{y:.10f}")
`}
  hint="2r−1=−0.5; α times that is −0.25; y=1.75."
  solution={`s = 2.0
alpha = 0.5
r = 0.25
y = s + alpha * (2.0 * r - 1.0)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'uses alpha', pattern: 'alpha|0\\.5', must: true, hint: 'include α(2r−1)'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'y', equals: '1.7500000000'},
  ]}
/>
