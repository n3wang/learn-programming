# 12.3 Entangled and separable states

> **Source:** Ideas from *Computational Physics* (Landau, Páez, Bordeianu), §12.3. Rewritten in original wording — **no** textbook `Entangle.py` dump. Continues [12.2b](./12-02b-qubits-labs). Next: [12.3b](./12-03b-entanglement-labs).

A state built as a tensor product $|\psi_A\rangle\otimes|\psi_B\rangle$ is **separable**. If two qubits interact so the joint state **cannot** be written as such a product, they are **entangled**. Distant spins in a total-spin-zero state remain correlated — measuring one constrains the other (Bell / Nobel 2022 context).

## Separability test and Bell states

For $|\Psi\rangle=[w,x,y,z]^T$ in the computational basis, the state is separable **iff** a simple amplitude condition holds. Bell states fail that test. Pick $wz=xy$ and $|\beta_{00}\rangle$ among lookalikes.

<GuidedChoiceExplanation preset="qc-entanglement-bell" />

Product states satisfy the test identically. The four Bell states are the usual maximally entangled basis (sign and pairing variants of $|00\rangle\pm|11\rangle$ and $|01\rangle\pm|10\rangle$).

## Density matrix (preview)

$$
\rho=\sum_i p_i\,|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|
$$

encodes ensembles of pure states (useful later for mixed / noisy QC).

## Pauli gates (QC names)

$$
X=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix},\ 
Y=\begin{bmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{bmatrix},\ 
Z=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Actions: $X|0\rangle=|1\rangle$, $X|1\rangle=|0\rangle$, $Z|0\rangle=|0\rangle$, $Z|1\rangle=-|1\rangle$, etc.

## Physics: two dipoles

Dipole–dipole $H\propto \boldsymbol\sigma_A\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma_B-3(\boldsymbol\sigma_A\cdot\hat r)(\boldsymbol\sigma_B\cdot\hat r)$. For $\hat r=\hat z$,

$$
H=\frac{\mu^2}{r^3}\bigl(X\otimes X+Y\otimes Y+Z\otimes Z-3\,Z\otimes Z\bigr)
=\frac{\mu^2}{r^3}
\begin{bmatrix}-2&0&0&0\\0&2&2&0\\0&2&2&0\\0&0&0&-2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Eigenvalues of $H/(\mu^2/r^3)$: $4,0,-2,-2$. Eigenvectors include Bell-like

$$
\frac{|01\rangle+|10\rangle}{\sqrt2},\quad |11\rangle,\quad |00\rangle,\quad \frac{|01\rangle-|10\rangle}{\sqrt2}
$$

— some separable, some entangled. Labs lock matrix entries and the separability measure.

## Takeaway

| Idea | Test / fact |
| --- | --- |
| Separable | $wz=xy$ for two-qubit amplitudes |
| Bell | Maximally entangled examples |
| $X,Y,Z$ | Single-qubit Pauli gates |
| Dipoles | $H$ in $\otimes$ space; mixed eigenstates |
