# 8.6 Friction, resonances, and beats

> **Source:** Ideas from *Computational Physics* (Landau, Páez, Bordeianu), §8.6. Original wording. Continues [8.5b](./08-04b-nonlinear-oscillation-labs). Labs: [8.6b](./08-05b-friction-resonance-labs).

## Viscous friction

Add drag linear in velocity, classify free regimes, and extend the dynamic-form acceleration $f_1$. Pick each equation among lookalikes.

<GuidedChoiceExplanation preset="friction-damping-regimes" />

| Regime | Condition | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Underdamped | $b < 2m\omega_0$ | decaying oscillation |
| Critical | $b = 2m\omega_0$ | fastest non-oscillatory return |
| Overdamped | $b > 2m\omega_0$ | slow creep, no ringing |

A harmonic drive $F_0\cos(\omega t)$ produces a steady response peaked near $\omega_0$ when damping is light. Transient + steady terms with nearby frequencies produce **beats** at $|\omega-\omega_0|/(2\pi)$ (labs: $\omega=2\pi\cdot 1.1$, $\omega_0=2\pi$ ⇒ $0.1$). Nonlinear restoring forces warp the resonance curve once linear friction/drive is under control.
