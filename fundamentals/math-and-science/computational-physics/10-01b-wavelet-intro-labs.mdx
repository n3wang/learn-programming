<Exercise title="10.1b.1 First regime" anchor="exercise-cp-101-y025">

:::tip Activity: $y(0.25)$
For the piecewise signal (10.1), print $y(0.25)$ with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="y(0.25)"
  heading="exercise-cp-101-y025"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="Piecewise y; print y(0.25) with 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`1.0000000000`}
  starter={`import math

t = 0.25
y = 0.0  # TODO: first regime sin(2π t)
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{y:.10f}")
`}
  hint="0≤t≤2 → sin(2πt); at 0.25 → 1."
  solution={`import math

t = 0.25
y = math.sin(2.0 * math.pi * t)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'sin', pattern: 'sin', must: true, hint: 'use sin(2πt)'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'y025', equals: '1.0000000000'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>

<Exercise title="10.1b.2 Second regime" anchor="exercise-cp-101-y225">

<CodeExercise
  title="y(2.25)"
  heading="exercise-cp-101-y225"

  filename="main.py"

y = 0.0  # TODO
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{y:.10f}")
`}
  hint="sin(2π·2.25)=1, sin(4π·2.25)=0 → 5."

`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'two sines', pattern: 'sin', must: true, hint: 'both 2π and 4π terms'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'y225', equals: '5.0000000000'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>

<Exercise title="10.1b.3 Third regime" anchor="exercise-cp-101-y825">

:::tip Activity: $y(8.25)$
Print the three-tone value at $t=8.25$ with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="y(8.25)"
  heading="exercise-cp-101-y825"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="Third regime at t=8.25; 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`-7.5000000000`}
  starter={`import math

t = 8.25
y = 0.0  # TODO: 2.5 sin2πt + 6 sin4πt + 10 sin6πt
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{y:.10f}")
`}
  hint="Values 1, 0, −1 → 2.5−10=−7.5."
  solution={`import math

t = 8.25
y = (
    2.5 * math.sin(2.0 * math.pi * t)
    + 6.0 * math.sin(4.0 * math.pi * t)
    + 10.0 * math.sin(6.0 * math.pi * t)
)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'three tones', pattern: '6\\.0|10\\.0|2\\.5', must: true, hint: 'use all three amplitudes'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'y825', equals: '-7.5000000000'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>

<Exercise title="10.1b.4 Mexican hat at 0" anchor="exercise-cp-101-mex0">

:::tip Activity: $\Psi(0)$
For $\sigma=1$, print the Mexican-hat $\Psi(0)=(1-t^2/\sigma^2)e^{-t^2/(2\sigma^2)}$ at $t=0$ with **10 decimals**.

No input.

<CodeExercise
  title="Mexican hat at 0"
  heading="exercise-cp-101-mex0"
  lang="python"
  filename="main.py"
  prompt="σ=1, t=0; print Mexican-hat Ψ; 10 decimals."
  sampleLog={`1.0000000000`}
  starter={`import math

t, sigma = 0.0, 1.0
psi = 0.0  # TODO
`}
  wrapSuffix={`
print(f"{psi:.10f}")
`}
  hint="(1-0)*e^0 = 1."
  solution={`import math

t, sigma = 0.0, 1.0
psi = (1.0 - (t / sigma) ** 2) * math.exp(-0.5 * (t / sigma) ** 2)
`}
  sourceChecks={[
    {name: 'exp', pattern: 'exp', must: true, hint: 'Gaussian factor'},
  ]}
  tests={[
    {name: 'mex0', equals: '1.0000000000'},
  ]}
/>

:::

</Exercise>
