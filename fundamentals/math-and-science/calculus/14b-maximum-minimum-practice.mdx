## 19. Cone about a sphere

**Book.** Sphere radius $8$; apex-to-center $y>8$. Similar triangles give
$x/8=(y+8)/\sqrt{y^{2}-64}$, hence
$V=\dfrac{64\pi(y+8)^{2}}{3(y-8)}$.
Then $V'\propto(y+8)(y-24)/(y-8)^{2}$; the relevant critical number is $y=24$. Cone height $y+8=32$ in, base radius $x=8\sqrt{2}$ in. Unique critical number on $(8,\infty)$ plus a first-derivative sign change (or second-derivative test on $V$) shows the volume is minimized.

## 20. Max-area rectangle in a parabola

**Book.** Inscribe $PBB'P'$ in $y^{2}=4px$ cut by $x=a$. With corner $(x,y)$,
$A=2y(a-x)=2ay-y^{3}/(2p)$, so $A'=2a-3y^{2}/(2p)=0$ gives $y=\sqrt{4ap/3}$.
Dimensions: height $2y=\dfrac{4}{3}\sqrt{3ap}$, depth $a-x=2a/3$. Since $A''=-3y/p<0$ and the critical number is unique, this is the maximum area.

## 21. Max-volume cylinder in a sphere

**Book.** Cylinder inscribed in a sphere of radius $R$: $r^{2}+h^{2}=R^{2}$, $V=2\pi r^{2}h$.
From $2r+2h\,dh/dr=0$ get $dh/dr=-r/h$; then $dV/dr=0$ yields $r^{2}=2h^{2}$, so $h=R/\sqrt{3}$ and full height $2h=2R/\sqrt{3}$.
