## 1. Prove $\lim_{\theta\to 0}\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\theta}=1$

The quotient is even, so treat $\theta\to 0^{+}$. On the unit circle, sector $COD$ ≤ $\triangle COB$ ≤ sector $AOB$ yields

$$
\cos\theta\le\frac{\sin\theta}{\theta}\le\frac{1}{\cos\theta}\xrightarrow{\theta\to 0^{+}}1.
$$

## 2. Prove $D_x(\sin x)=\cos x$

$$
\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}
=\cos x\cdot\frac{\sin\Delta x}{\Delta x}
+\sin x\cdot\frac{\cos\Delta x-1}{\Delta x}
\to\cos x.
$$

## 3. Prove $\tan'={\sec}^{2}$ and $\sec'=\tan\sec$

**(a)** Quotient rule on $\sin/\cos$.

**(b)** Two routes: reciprocal rule on $1/\cos$, or differentiate $\tan^{2}x+1=\sec^{2}x$:

$$
2\tan x\sec^{2}x=2\sec x\cdot D_x(\sec x)\implies D_x(\sec x)=\tan x\sec x.
$$

## 4–5. Period of $\tan$ and $\tan(u-v)$

$\sin(x+\pi)=-\sin x$, $\cos(x+\pi)=-\cos x$ ⇒ $\tan(x+\pi)=\tan x$.

Divide $\sin(u-v)/\cos(u-v)$ by $\cos u\cos v$:

$$
\tan(u-v)=\frac{\tan u-\tan v}{1+\tan u\tan v}.
$$

(The plus formula flips signs: $(\tan u+\tan v)/(1-\tan u\tan v)$.)

## 9–12. Higher / implicit derivatives

**Book 9.** $y=x\sin x$ ⇒ $y'''=-x\cos x-3\sin x$.

**Book 10.** $y=\tan^{2}(3x-2)$ ⇒ first $y'=6\tan(\cdots)\sec^{2}(\cdots)$; then product for $y''$.

**Book 11.** $y=\sin(x+y)$ ⇒ $y'=\dfrac{\cos(x+y)}{1-\cos(x+y)}$.

**Book 12.** $\sin y+\cos x=1$ ⇒ $y'=\sin x/\cos y$; differentiate again for $y''$.

## 13. Related rates — pilot sighting

Altitude $2$ mi, ground distance $x=2\cot\theta$, $dx/dt=-240$ mi/h. At $\theta=30°$,

$$
\frac{dx}{dt}=-2\csc^{2}\theta\frac{d\theta}{dt}\implies\frac{d\theta}{dt}=30\text{ rad/h}.
$$

## 16. Absolute extrema of $\sin x+x$ on $[0,2\pi]$

$f'=\cos x+1=0$ ⇒ $x=\pi$. Table: $f(0)=0$, $f(\pi)=\pi$, $f(2\pi)=2\pi$ ⇒ abs min $0$ at $0$; abs max $2\pi$ at $2\pi$.
