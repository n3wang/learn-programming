## 1. Prove $D_x(\sec^{-1}x)=\dfrac{1}{x\sqrt{x^{2}-1}}$

Let $y=\sec^{-1}x$, so $\sec y=x$. Implicit differentiation:

$$
\tan y\cdot\sec y\cdot y'=1.
$$

$\tan^{2}y=\sec^{2}y-1=x^{2}-1$. On this chapter’s range ($[0,\pi/2)\cup[\pi,3\pi/2)$), $\tan y\ge 0$, so $\tan y=\sqrt{x^{2}-1}$. Hence

$$
y'=\frac{1}{\tan y\cdot\sec y}=\frac{1}{x\sqrt{x^{2}-1}}.
$$

## 2–4. Chain rule with $\arcsin$, $\arccos$, $\arctan$

**Book.** $\arcsin(2x-3)$, $\arccos(x^{2})$, $\arctan(3x^{2})$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
D_x\arcsin(2x-3)&=\frac{2}{\sqrt{1-(2x-3)^{2}}},\\
D_x\arccos(x^{2})&=-\frac{2x}{\sqrt{1-x^{4}}},\\
D_x\arctan(3x^{2})&=\frac{6x}{1+9x^{4}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

## 12. $\arcsin(\sin x)$ vs the identity

**(a)** $\sin(\arcsin y)=y$ by definition (for $y\in[-1,1]$).

**(b)** $\arcsin(\sin\pi)=\arcsin 0=0$ — **not** $\pi$.

**(c)** $\arcsin(\sin x)=x$ **iff** $x\in[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$.

## 14. Mural — maximize subtended angle

Mural height $12$ ft; bottom $6$ ft above eye. Distance $x$ from the wall:

$$
\tan\theta=\frac{12x}{x^{2}+108},\qquad
\theta=\arctan\!\left(\frac{12x}{x^{2}+108}\right).
$$

$\theta'=0$ at $x=6\sqrt{3}\approx 10.4$ ft (relative max by the first-derivative test).

### 21–24. Products that simplify

**Book answers.**

- $y=x^{2}\arccos(2/x)$ ⇒ $y'=2x\big(\arccos(2/x)+1/\sqrt{x^{2}-4}\big)$

### 33 / 36. Relating inverse functions

$\arcsin x=\arctan\big(x/\sqrt{1-x^{2}}\big)$ for $|x|<1$ (limits at the endpoints).

For this chapter’s $\sec^{-1}$: $\sec^{-1}x=\arccos(1/x)$ when $x\ge 1$, and $\sec^{-1}x=2\pi-\arccos(1/x)$ when $x\le -1$.
