## 1. Spherical balloon

Gas escapes at $2$ ft³/min. Surface shrink rate when $r=12$:

$dV/dt=-2=4\pi r^{2}\,dr/dt$, $dS/dt=8\pi r\,dr/dt=-4/r=-1/3$ ft²/min.

## 2. Conical funnel

Similar triangles $r=h/2$; $V=\pi h^{3}/12$. With $dV/dt=-1$ and $h=6$ (2 in from top of an 8 in funnel):

$$
\frac{dh}{dt}=-\frac{1}{9\pi}\ \mathrm{in/s}.
$$

## 3. Sand pile

$h=(4/3)r$ ⇒ $V=(4/9)\pi r^{3}$, $dV/dt=(4/3)\pi r^{2}\,dr/dt$.

**(a)** $r=3$, $dr/dt=1/4$ (ft/min) ⇒ $dV/dt=3\pi$.

**(b)** $r=6$, $dV/dt=24$ ⇒ $dr/dt=1/(2\pi)$.

## 5. Rectangle of constant area

$xy=50$, $dx/dt=2$. Then $x\,dy/dt+y\cdot 2=0$ and $dP/dt=2(dx/dt+dy/dt)$.

- $x=5$: $dP/dt=-4$ (decreasing)
- $x=10$: $dP/dt=+2$ (**increasing**)
- Ceases decreasing when the figure is a square of side $5\sqrt{2}$

:::tip Book wording
Some keys say “decreasing” at $x=10$; the rate $+2$ means the perimeter is increasing.
:::

## 6. When $dS/dt=dr/dt$

For $S=4\pi r^{2}$: $8\pi r=1$ ⇒ $r=1/(8\pi)$.

## 7. Pulley and truck

Rope $50$ ft; pulley $20$ ft up; truck hitch $2$ ft up. When weight raised $x=6$ and truck speed $dy/dt=9$:

$$
\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac92\sqrt{3}\ \mathrm{ft/s}.
$$

## 8. Shadow tip

Object height $h$, light height $H$, object speed $v$: tip speed

$$
V=\frac{Hv}{H-h}.
$$

As $h\to 0$, $V\to v$; as $h\to H$, $V\to+\infty$.

## Supplementary Problems

### 9–11. Trough, cylinder, walking shadow

Trough rise $1/8$ ft/min; cylinder rise $2/(9\pi)$ ft/min; man/light: tip $16/3$, shadow length $4/3$ ft/s.

### 12–16. Balloon, ladder, cone, barge, kite

Balloon–ground distance $12$ ft/s; $20$ ft ladder at $x=12$: top falls $3/2$ ft/s; cone reservoir answers involve $\pi$ in the denominator; barge cable $9/13$ ft/s; kite string $16$ ft/s.

### 17–21. Trains, falling ball shadow, ships, leaky cistern, filter

Trains separate at $\frac{105\sqrt{2}}{2}$ mi/h at 3 p.m.; ball shadow $200$ ft/s after $1$ s; leak rate $(10-3\pi)$ ft³/min; cylinder rises $4/9$ in/min under the filter.
