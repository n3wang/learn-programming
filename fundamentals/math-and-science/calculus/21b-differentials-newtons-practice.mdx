## 1. Linear approximations

**(a)** $\sqrt[3]{124}$: $x=125$, $\Delta x=-1$, $f'(125)=1/75$

$$
\sqrt[3]{124}\sim 5-\frac1{75}=\frac{374}{75}\approx 4.9867
$$

(true $\approx 4.9866$).

**(b)** $\sin 61^\circ$: use radians — $x=\pi/3$, $\Delta x=\pi/180$, $f'=\cos(\pi/3)=1/2$

$$
\sin 61^\circ\sim \frac{\sqrt3}{2}+\frac{\pi}{360}\approx 0.8747
$$

(true $\approx 0.8746$).

:::tip Radians
Trig derivatives in calculus assume the argument is in **radians**. Convert $1^\circ=\pi/180$.
:::

## 2. Cube volume, $1\%$ side increase

$V=x^{3}$, $\Delta x=0.01x$:

$$
\Delta V\sim 3x^{2}\cdot(0.01x)=0.03x^{3}.
$$

Volume grows by roughly **$3\%$**.

## 4. $dy/dx$ via differentials

**(a)** $xy+x-2y=5$ ⇒ $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-\dfrac{y+1}{x-2}$.

**(b)** $\dfrac{2x}{y}-\dfrac{3y}{x}=8$ ⇒ $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{y}{x}$ (after canceling $2x^{2}+3y^{2}\neq 0$).

**(c)** Parametric $x=3\cos\theta-\cos 3\theta$, $y=3\sin\theta-\sin 3\theta$:

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{\cos\theta-\cos 3\theta}{-\sin\theta+\sin 3\theta}.
$$

## 5. Newton: $x^{3}+2x-5=0$

One real root between $1$ and $2$. With $x_0=1$:

$$
x_{n+1}=\frac{2x_n^{3}+5}{3x_n^{2}+2}.
$$

$x_1=1.4$; stabilizes at $\approx 1.328268856$.

---

## 6. Newton: $2\cos x-x^{2}=0$

Even $f$ ⇒ roots $\pm r$. From $x_0=1$:

$$
x_{n+1}=\frac{x_n^{2}+2(x_n\sin x_n+\cos x_n)}{2(x_n+\sin x_n)},
$$

converging to $r\approx 1.02168995$ (and $-r$).

### 11–16. Measurement error and surfaces

- **11.** $v=\sqrt{64.4 h}$, $h=100\pm 0.5$ ⇒ $|\Delta v|\approx 0.2$ ft/s.
- **12.** Flight $2$ mi above equator: extra $\approx 2\pi\cdot 2\approx 12.6$ mi.
- **13.** $|dr|\le 0.001$, need $|dA|\le 0.1$ ⇒ $r\le 16$ in.
- **14.** $pV=20$, $p=5\pm 0.02$ ⇒ $V=4\pm 0.016$.
- **15.** $F=1/r^{2}$, $F=4\pm 0.05$ ⇒ $r=0.5\pm 0.003$.
- **16.** Cone total surface $S=\pi r\sqrt{r^{2}+h^{2}}+\pi r^{2}$:
  - (a) fixed $r$: $dS=\dfrac{\pi r h}{\sqrt{r^{2}+h^{2}}}\,\Delta h$
  - (b) fixed $h$: $dS=\pi\!\left(\dfrac{h^{2}+2r^{2}}{\sqrt{r^{2}+h^{2}}}+2r\right)\Delta r$
