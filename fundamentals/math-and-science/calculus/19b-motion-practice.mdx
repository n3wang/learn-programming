## 4. Speed vs velocity

**Book.** $v=2(t-1)^{2}(2t-5)$, $a=12(t-1)(t-2)$.

## Supplementary Problems

### 9. Prove the speed criterion

Speed $S=|v|$. If $v>0$ then $S=v$ and $dS/dt=a$. If $v<0$ then $S=-v$ and $dS/dt=-a$. So $S$ increases when $v$ and $a$ have the **same** sign, and decreases when they have **opposite** signs.
