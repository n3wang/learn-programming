**Claim.** For $f(x)=ax^{3}+bx^{2}+cx+d$, the inflection abscissa is $-b/(3a)$. It equals the average of the two critical numbers when both exist, or the single critical number when the discriminant of $f'$ vanishes.

### 16. Symmetry ⇒ even / odd

**Book hints.** $y$-axis symmetry ⇒ $(-x,f(x))$ on the graph ⇒ $f(-x)=f(x)$. Origin symmetry ⇒ $(-x,-f(x))$ ⇒ odd.

### 17. Prove Theorem 15.1 (concavity)

**Idea.** $f''>0$ ⇒ $f'$ increasing near $x_0$; MVT compares $f(x)$ with the tangent $f(x_0)+f'(x_0)(x-x_0)$; the curve lies above the tangent. Part (b) via $-f$.
