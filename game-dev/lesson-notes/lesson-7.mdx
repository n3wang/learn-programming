# The future and beyond

## 7.1 Moving the game forward

:::caution Instructor question
If you finished the assignments, you should have **three** valuable things:

- A **completed assignment** you can share on Discord for feedback. Acting on feedback is one of the fastest ways to improve.
- A **card game** you can keep expanding with the tools from the course. Repeat: add mechanics/systems → playtest → balance → document, until you are happy. It can be a portfolio piece.
- **Knowledge of professional practice**: proposal, documentation, balancing, diagramming, testing, and more. You used tools, theories, and techniques working designers use every day.

Keep developing this game, or start a new one from scratch using the same steps.
:::

:::tip Gabriel’s tip
Share the finished assignment on **Discord** if you want instructor and community feedback. See you there: [https://discord.gg/VA2M5EHyjj](https://discord.gg/VA2M5EHyjj)
:::

:::info Reflection opportunity
Looking back to where you started: how do you feel about your craft as a game designer now? Where did you improve most? What do you still need to work on?
:::

## 7.2 Sharing your companion work

:::caution Instructor question
Share your work on Discord so instructors and the community can comment.

On **this site**, answers live in your browser. To share:

1. Copy the text from each answer box into a document, **or** export screenshots of the lesson pages.
2. Paste a public link (Google Doc, published page, or similar) in Discord.
3. Ask for feedback.

If you used the original Notion companion instead:

1. Press **Share** (top right)
2. Open the **Publish** tab
3. **Publish to web**
4. Turn **Allow comments** on; turn the other options off
5. Copy the web link and post it on Discord
:::
