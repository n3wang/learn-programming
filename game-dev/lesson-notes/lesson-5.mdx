## 5.2 Balancing Tiny Pixel Fights

:::caution Instructor question
You could build a combat model from scratch; here we jump to balancing.

The base game is very deterministic, so it is less fun once you have “cracked the code.” Change numbers and add a bit of randomness if you have not already.

In the spreadsheet, **only edit cells with an orange background**. Other cells are calculated from those.

The file has three sections:

- **Stats** — default stats for all cards
- **Combat Matrix** — result of every matchup
- **Combat Results** — counts and percentages from the matrix. Win % helps you spot overpowered (OP) cards

Change orange values until **you** think they suit your game. Paste screenshots of all three sections below (or describe the numbers if you cannot paste images here).
:::

:::tip Gabriel’s tip
You may have added mechanics already. Modelling them is optional. Spreadsheets take time if you are new; you can come back later, especially if you keep developing the game after the course.
:::

:::info Reflection opportunity
Balancing can make or break a game. Even huge companies ship poorly balanced games. Why is that so common? (Hint: it relates to the next section.)
:::

## 5.3 Playtesting your balance

:::caution Instructor question
You now have a better idea of fixed, random, and procedural elements, plus your 5.2 reflection.

Use that, and the process from Lesson 2, and **playtest the values**. Results may surprise you. If you changed values, paste or describe the updated **STATS** section.

This is a good time to use the interactive PDF if you prefer a digital play surface.
:::

:::tip Gabriel’s tip
If you re-balance, **go wild**. Tiny tweaks teach less than big swings. If your hypothesis was right you will land in the middle; if not, you still get a better balance.
:::

:::info Reflection opportunity
Would a 100% symmetric game (all characters the same stats, “perfectly balanced”) be more engaging than asymmetry or imbalance? If every character has the same chance to win by the numbers, is that more fun? Could special powers fix a problem? **Try this by changing stats.**
:::
