# Game Design companion

You are already a student of **Game Design Thinking**’s course **Game Design: Basic Tools, Theories and Techniques**. Congratulations!

This companion site contains activities and assignments that help you get the most out of the course. By the end, you will have a **card game** you can play and enjoy. You will craft the mechanics from scratch, playtest them, document them, and more.

Join the **Discord** channel to share your work and get feedback from the instructor:

[https://discord.gg/VA2M5EHyjj](https://discord.gg/VA2M5EHyjj)

Following these activities will increase your learning outcome. They are designed so you can put the course into practice and design like a professional game designer.

## How to use this site

1. Work through the lessons in order from the sidebar.
2. Type answers in the orange **instructor** prompts. They are saved **in this browser** on this device.
3. You can also answer in a notebook or PDF. If you do not use this site, the instructor will not be able to give you the same kind of in-page feedback.

If you still have a Notion copy of the original companion, you may keep using it. This site is the same assignment, split into lessons.
