A **boolean** is `True` or `False`. Compare numbers with `==`, `!=`, `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=`.

| Operator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `==` | Equal |
| `!=` | Not equal |
| `>` | Greater |
| `<` | Less |

## Logical operators

`and` needs both sides true. `or` needs at least one true. `not` flips the value.
