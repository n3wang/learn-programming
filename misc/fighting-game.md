title: Fighting Game
---

A little 2-player fighting game built with plain JS Canvas — samurai vs. ninja, best of one round, keyboard controls only (no touch support).

**Controls**

| Player | Move | Attack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 (Samurai) | A / D | W |
| P2 (Ninja) | ← / → | ↓ |
